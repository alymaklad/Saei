Dashboard Search Settings CV Email Reports Features

Search

Pulls jobs from Greenhouse and Lever's public APIs, an optional Google Sheets watchlist, sites you add yourself, and SerpAPI's free tier for Google Jobs. Set a position/title and add job-board links on the Search page. Every job is deduped on URL before anything else happens to it.

ATS scoring

Combines deterministic keyword overlap (how real ATS filters actually work) with LLM judgment on seniority and domain fit, using whichever provider is set on the Settings page.

CV rewriting

When the fit score is low, the CV gets reframed around the job description's real requirements — never fabricated. Missing skills are reported separately rather than invented into the CV. Download every tailored copy from the CV page.

Draft for review

Every board outside the whitelist gets a complete draft — CV, cover letter, target URL — held for your approval instead of being sent automatically.

Email

Sends application emails through your own Gmail account via OAuth — no per-email cost, nothing routed through a third party. Connect your account and see everything sent on the Email page.

Skill gap tracking

Tallies which required skills show up as "missing" most often across recent jobs, surfaced on the Dashboard.

Reports

A daily summary of what happened and a weekly news digest for your field, delivered over a free Telegram bot. Full send history on the Reports page.
