Requires a Gmail OAuth client file at credentials/gmail_credentials.json (Google Cloud Console → OAuth client ID → Desktop app). Clicking Connect opens a new browser tab where you sign in to your Google account and grant access — once you do, the tab tells you to close it and this page updates to "Connected" automatically. Nothing is typed here; Google issues the credentials directly to this app and they're saved to credentials/gmail_token.json. Times out after 3 minutes if the tab is left unfinished.
